# FINCAP GUARD — STRESS TESTING & SCENARIO ANALYSIS POLICY
Document ID: POL-STR-2026-V1
Classification: Internal Governance Framework

## Section 1: Stress Testing Mandate
Stress testing evaluates the resilience of portfolio capital against low-probability, high-severity market dislocations. Portfolio managers must simulate stressed losses across both historical crises and forward-looking economic scenarios before authorizing risk-budget expansions.

## Section 2: Core Stress Scenario Catalog
FINCAP Guard maintains four standardized baseline stress scenarios:
1. **Market Crash**: A 20.0% sudden drop in broad Indian equity markets (NIFTY 50), accompanied by a 5.0% flight to gold, a 5.0% softening in sovereign yields, and an 8.0% haircut on corporate credit.
2. **Inflation Shock**: A sudden stagflationary surge triggering a 10.0% decline in equities, an 8.0% rally in physical gold, a 7.0% sell-off in government bonds, and cash drag erosion of 2.0%.
3. **Interest Rate Shock**: Aggressive benchmark policy rate tightening producing a 12.0% loss in sovereign debt duration, a 10.0% drop in corporate paper, and a 10.0% contraction in equity multiples.
4. **Systemic Liquidity Crisis**: Comprehensive market freeze impairing secondary liquidity across equities (-15.0%), fixed income (-10.0%), and cash conversion delays (-20.0%).

## Section 3: Stressed Loss Tolerances & Escalation Thresholds
- **Maximum Tolerated Stress Loss**: No single stress scenario may induce a simulated portfolio loss exceeding 12.0% of total capital.
- If simulated loss surpasses 12.0%, the Control Safeguard Engine automatically flags a Stress Tolerance Breach, requiring dynamic rebalancing to reduce beta and duration exposure.
