## Section 1: Basel III Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) Institutional Analogy
In international banking supervision, the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS) mandates the Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR). The objective of the LCR is to promote the short-term resilience of the liquidity risk profile by ensuring banks maintain an unencumbered pool of High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA) capable of meeting net cash outflows over a 30-day severe stress horizon.

## Section 2: Important Hackathon Architecture Disclaimer
FINCAP Guard adapts institutional concepts—such as the Liquid Asset Ratio (LAR) and 30-day liquidity horizons—into configurable internal risk boundaries. While inspired by regulatory principles (such as Basel III LCR and SEBI mutual fund stress guidelines), internal thresholds (e.g., our 20.0% minimum liquidity rule or 40.0% equity cap) are configurable organizational policies designed for demonstrative decision safeguarding, rather than universal statutory mandates.
